Hyper Law Update Request

New Intake Option

Add a new intake path called:

“Upload Existing Complaint”

Users who already have a complaint drafted should be able to upload it instead of answering all incident intake questions.

Accepted formats:

* PDF
* DOCX
* TXT

After upload, Hyper Law should:

1. Extract facts
2. Extract parties
3. Extract dates
4. Extract claims
5. Extract exhibits
6. Build a case profile automatically
7. Route the case into the Tutor system

Tutor Graduation System

Users should NOT immediately have access to Battle Documents.

The Battle Document button must remain locked until the system determines the case is sufficiently developed.

Display:

“🔒 Battle Documents Locked”

“Graduate from the Hyper Tutor Program to unlock advanced litigation drafting.”

⸻

Graduation Requirements

The Tutor should evaluate:

Case Knowledge

Can the user clearly explain:

* What happened
* Who was involved
* When it happened
* Where it happened
* Why they believe their rights were violated

Evidence Completion

Evaluate whether the case contains:

* Documents
* Photos
* Videos
* Audio
* Witnesses
* Reports
* Admissions

Claim Support

Every claim should be linked to supporting evidence.

The system should identify unsupported claims and missing evidence.

⸻

Readiness Engine

Create a Readiness Score.

Purpose:
Measure whether enough information exists to generate high-quality litigation documents.

Readiness Categories:

* 0–39% = Not Ready
* 40–69% = Needs Development
* 70–89% = Litigation Ready
* 90–99% = Battle Ready

Visual:
Animated gavel that becomes stronger as readiness increases.

⸻

Case Strength Engine

Separate from Readiness.

A case can be:

* Ready but weak
* Strong but incomplete

The Strength Engine should evaluate:

* Evidence quality
* Corroboration
* Consistency
* Contradictions
* Admissions
* Official records
* Timeline reliability

Visual:
Animated Scale of Justice.

The scale should visually compare support for the user’s position versus contrary evidence.

⸻

Strength Categories

* Weak
* Moderate
* Strong
* Very Strong

Display supporting reasons such as:

✓ Video evidence

✓ Official records

✓ Independent witnesses

✓ Admissions

✓ Consistent timeline

⸻

Guidance Section

Provide constructive feedback explaining:

* What evidence is missing
* What evidence would strengthen the case
* Which claims appear well-supported
* Which claims need additional support

The goal is education and preparation, not legal advice.

⸻

Required Disclaimer

Display on all scoring screens:

“Hyper Law does not provide legal advice, legal representation, legal predictions, or legal outcomes. All readiness and strength scores are informational estimates generated by artificial intelligence and are not guarantees of success.”

⸻

Design Goal

The purpose of these systems is to prevent unnecessary document generation, reduce AI resource usage, improve document quality, and encourage users to fully develop their cases before advanced litigation tools become available.
