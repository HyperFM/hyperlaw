Don’t try to upload everything you know.  That would eventually become outdated and hard to maintain.  Instead build a knowledge library.  Think of it like Wikipedia for your app.  Every law should exist as an object.  Example: {   "title": "First Amendment Retaliation",   "citation": "42 U.S.C. §1983",   "elements": [     "Protected speech",     "Adverse action",     "Causal connection"   ],   "keywords": [     "retaliation",     "speech",     "complaint",     "criticized"   ],   "commonEvidence": [     "Bodycam",     "Emails",     "Witnesses"   ] } Then the AI simply searches that library and recommends relevant claims based on the facts you’ve entered.  ⸻  Even Better: Build a Rule Engine  Instead of having AI guess, let the app apply rules.  Example:  IF  * User says there is a policy. * Policy was acknowledged. * Employee acted differently.  THEN  Suggest:  * Policy Violation * Deliberate Indifference * Fabrication  Another:  IF  * Statement A * Statement B * Evidence disproves one of them  THEN  Suggest:  * Contradiction Screen * Credibility Screen * False Narrative  This is much more reliable than relying entirely on AI.  ⸻  One final idea that I think would make Hyper Screen really stand out: create a Case Health Dashboard.
