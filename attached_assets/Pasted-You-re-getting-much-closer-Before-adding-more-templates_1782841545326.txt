⸻

One other feature I would add early—because I think it would become one of the app’s biggest strengths—is an “Evidence Vault.” Instead of asking you to retype quotes or facts into every screen, you’d import your videos, reports, photos, and transcripts once. Then, while building a screen, you could click “Insert Evidence,” select a quote, timestamp, image, or document excerpt, and the builder would automatically fill in the relevant fields. That avoids duplicate work and keeps every screen tied back to the original evidence, which is incredibly valuable as a case grows.
